These observations summarize backed-up reports; they are not new measurements. The private archive preserves the original reports and raw evidence. Public summaries omit manufacturer excerpts, full prompts, model dumps and session details.

- Manual-PDF experiment: six PDFs, controlled section extraction, fixed A 300/45 and B 500/75 approximate-token chunking, and structure-aware C around 800 tokens. Manual extraction corruption/coverage concerns are historical findings, not proof of a current Pinecone defect.
- Original-guide experiment: **36/24/12** chunks; saved positive guide recall@5 **1.000/1.000/1.000**. Retained JSON reports provide per-question source IDs and timing. Recall does not establish answer accuracy.
- Phase 4L controlled model comparison (2026-10-25): `Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507` versus `Qwen/Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507`, exactly three fixed-input questions each, temperature 0, 2,400-token budget, identical evidence/schema/setup per pair. The sample did not show a clear grounded-quality benefit from switching; both had unsupported conceptual/diagnostic additions. Recommendation was to keep the current model, not a universal model ranking.

Raw outputs/catalogs remain private; the exact fixed-input benchmark is not independently replayable from this sanitized summary alone.
